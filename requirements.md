# AI-Powered Logistics Intelligence & Margin Optimization Platform

## 🎯 Demo Objective

Demonstrate a platform that enables:

- Unify warehousing, drayage, transportation, and fleet operations into one operational control layer
- Provide real-time visibility across inventory, containers, loads, lanes, and assets
- Improve margin control through shipment-level profitability, detention reduction, and cost intelligence
- Use AI for predictive demand planning, dispatch optimization, exception handling, and network balancing
- Support scalable operations across warehouse-anchored, multi-modal logistics workflows
- Give leadership a clearer operating view across acquired or expanding logistics businesses

---

## 1. Multi-Modal Logistics Command Center

**Goal:** Create a centralized control tower for drayage, warehousing, transportation, and network activity.

**Features**
- Unified live dashboard for: inbound loads, outbound shipments, containers, warehouse activity, drayage moves, fleet status
- Network-wide operational visibility by: warehouse, port / rail node, customer, lane, carrier
- Status tracking: booked, at port, in drayage, at warehouse, transloading, in linehaul, delivered, exception
- Control tower alerts for: missed appointments, port congestion, dwell risk, late pickups, delayed outbound loads
- Drill-down from network → facility → shipment → order → cost/margin view

**📌 Demo Moment**
Show a live map and network dashboard with active containers, warehouse loads, and drayage moves. One shipment is flagged for port dwell risk, another for warehouse bottleneck, and the system surfaces the downstream margin impact instantly.

---

## 2. Drayage & Container Visibility Engine

**Goal:** Improve control over container movement, port/rail execution, and dwell-related cost leakage.

**Features**
- Container-level tracking across: port pickup, rail ramp, chassis assignment, outgate / ingate, warehouse arrival, return empty
- Appointment and milestone visibility
- Dwell and detention tracking
- Free-time monitoring and alerting
- Container exception workflows for: rolled appointments, demurrage risk, driver delay, empty return issues
- ETA prediction for drayage legs
- Chassis and container utilization analytics

**📌 Demo Moment**
Display several live containers arriving through port and rail. The system predicts one container will exceed free time, recommends a re-sequenced dispatch plan, and quantifies the detention cost avoided.

---

## 3. Warehouse Intelligence & Flow Orchestration

**Goal:** Turn warehouse operations into a real-time planning and throughput system.

**Features**
- Live warehouse dashboard for: inbound receipts, dock activity, putaway, staging, picking, outbound readiness
- Dock scheduling and door utilization
- Yard-to-dock orchestration
- Real-time slotting and staging visibility
- Labor and throughput monitoring
- Inventory aging and velocity views
- Exception alerts for: dock congestion, late unloads, outbound delay risk, inventory mismatch
- Facility comparison across multiple sites

**📌 Demo Moment**
Show a 100,000+ sq ft warehouse view with inbound containers arriving from drayage, outbound orders waiting for staging, and an AI recommendation to rebalance dock assignments to prevent missed shipping windows. This directly fits the site's public mention of a 100,000+ sq ft warehouse expansion opportunity.

## 4. Predictive Demand Planning & Capacity Forecasting

**Goal:** Forecast network demand and align warehouse, drayage, and transportation capacity before service failures happen.

**Features**
- Demand forecasting by: customer, lane, facility, SKU / commodity group, seasonality pattern
- Capacity planning for: warehouse space, dock availability, fleet usage, carrier allocation, driver requirements
- Surge prediction based on: historical volume, order inflow, port schedules, customer activity
- Capacity shortfall alerts
- Scenario simulation: peak season, customer volume spike, delayed import wave, warehouse overflow risk

**📌 Demo Moment**
Simulate a demand spike from a large customer. The platform forecasts warehouse congestion, flags likely drayage shortfall, and recommends additional carrier capacity plus adjusted dock windows before the surge hits.

## 5. Shipment-Level Margin Optimization Engine

**Goal:** Give precise visibility into operational profitability, not just volume.

**Features**
- Margin analysis by: shipment, customer, lane, warehouse, drayage leg, carrier
- Cost allocation for: drayage, detention, fuel, warehouse labor, storage, linehaul, accessorials
- Margin erosion alerts
- Customer profitability segmentation
- "What changed margin?" diagnostics
- Recommendations for: repricing lanes, changing carrier mix, reducing unplanned storage, improving transload timing
- Gross margin forecast for active loads

**📌 Demo Moment**
Open a customer account and show how a lane appears profitable at a high level but becomes margin-negative after detention, rehandling, and storage costs. The system recommends pricing and operational changes to restore margin.

## 6. AI Dispatch, Routing & Load Planning

**Goal:** Improve movement efficiency across drayage and transportation networks.

**Features**
- AI-assisted dispatch planning
- Dynamic route and stop sequencing
- Load-to-carrier matching
- Drayage dispatch optimization by: appointment time, driver position, chassis availability, container urgency
- Predictive ETA updates
- Multi-leg shipment orchestration: port / rail → warehouse, warehouse → customer, warehouse → cross-dock / transload
- Empty miles and deadhead reduction suggestions

**📌 Demo Moment**
Show five active drayage moves and three outbound truckloads. The platform reassigns dispatch based on appointment risk and geography, improving on-time performance and reducing empty miles.

## 7. Exception Management & SLA Automation

**Goal:** Handle operational exceptions before they become customer-facing failures.

**Features**
- Real-time exception queue for: late arrivals, missed appointments, damaged freight, inventory mismatches, port delays, capacity gaps
- Severity scoring based on: SLA exposure, margin impact, customer priority, operational dependency
- Automated escalation workflows
- Resolution ownership tracking
- SLA timers and breach warnings
- Root-cause analysis categories for continuous improvement

**📌 Demo Moment**
A container delay threatens a same-day outbound order. The system raises the issue, assigns ownership, recommends alternate inventory or rescheduling, and updates the customer-service dashboard before an SLA breach occurs.
